### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Driving Profile Selection**
   - Select driving scenario: Urban Traffic Jam, Highway Cruising, or Aggressive Driving.

2. **Step 2: AI Assistance Mode Toggle**
   - Toggle AI Eco-Advisor ON/OFF to compare manual driving vs AI-guided speed optimization.

3. **Step 3: Monitor Telemetry**
   - Observe instantaneous power draw (kW), State of Charge (SOC %), and Eco-Score.

4. **Step 4: Performance Evaluation**
   - Compare Wh/km consumption between manual and AI eco-assisted runs.
