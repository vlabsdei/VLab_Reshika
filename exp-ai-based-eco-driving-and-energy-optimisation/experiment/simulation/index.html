Downhill Physics (Why is it a PASS with Negative Values?):
  1. Why F & P are negative: Downhill gravity pushes the vehicle forward. The motor does not need to pull the car, so tractive force (F) and power draw (P) drop below zero.
  2. Why E is negative: Instead of consuming battery power, the motor acts as a generator (regenerative braking) to capture kinetic energy and charge the battery. Hence, net energy consumed (E) is negative (energy is gained, not lost).
  3. Why it PASSES: Since the battery recovers charge rather than depleting it, the vehicle's driving range is extended beyond the baseline. Gaining energy is highly efficient, yielding a PASS verdict instead of a FAIL.

Force Breakdown (Ftotal = Fgravity + Frolling + Fdrag)

Fdrag = 0.5 × 1.225 × 0.24 × 2.2 × (16.67m/s)² = 89 N
Ftotal = 0 + 197 + 89 = 286 N

P = Ftotal × v
