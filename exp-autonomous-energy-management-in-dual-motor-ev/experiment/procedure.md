### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Set Vehicle Cargo Load**
   - Add driver and passenger/cargo blocks into trunk slots (1500 kg to 2000 kg).

2. **Step 2: Adjust Motor Power Targets**
   - Adjust P1 (Front) and P2 (Rear) motor sliders manually or enable Autonomous Mode.

3. **Step 3: Test Surface Friction**
   - Select Dry Asphalt ($\mu = 1.0$) or Ice Road ($\mu = 0.2$).

4. **Step 4: Monitor Slippage & Efficiency**
   - Inspect vector canvas for slip warnings and efficiency penalties.
