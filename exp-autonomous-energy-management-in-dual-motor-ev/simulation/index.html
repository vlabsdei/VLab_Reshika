Traction & Slip Physics Guide

Dynamic friction capacity depends on load distribution and surface friction coefficient:

Plimit = Loadaxle × μ × Scale

In Autonomous Mode, power is split matching dynamic load shift, optimizing traction. In Manual Mode, unequal allocation causes local slips, reducing efficiency.
