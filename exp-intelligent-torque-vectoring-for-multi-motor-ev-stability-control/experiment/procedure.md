### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Base Torque and Steering Configuration**
   - Adjust Base Motor Torque slider (50 Nm to 400 Nm).
   - Set Steering Angle slider (-45° to +45°).

2. **Step 2: Torque Vectoring Controller Setup**
   - Select Unvectored (50:50 Rigid Split) vs Intelligent Torque Vectoring Active.

3. **Step 3: Run Cornering Test**
   - Select Scenario A (Black Ice Curve) or Scenario B (Power Slide).
   - Observe tire smoke particles and yaw trajectory path.

4. **Step 4: Analyze Force Balance**
   - Inspect inner vs outer wheel grip limits and telemetry logs.
