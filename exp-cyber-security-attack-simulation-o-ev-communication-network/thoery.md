# THEORY
## 1. Objective
The objective of this laboratory experiment is to analyze the electro-mechanical power flow dynamics of a dual-motor Electric Vehicle (EV) powertrain while evaluating operational resilience against cyber-physical attacks. Students will simulate malicious Controller Area Network (CAN) message injections via a vulnerable High-Voltage Charging Port and measure the mitigation effectiveness of modern automotive security frameworks.

---

## 2. Basic Terminologies
* **Powertrain:** The collection of components in a vehicle that generate and deliver power to the road surface, including the battery pack, inverters, electric motors, and transaxles.
* **Controller Area Network (CAN) Bus:** A robust vehicle bus standard designed to allow Electronic Control Units (ECUs) to communicate with each other efficiently without a central host computer.
* **State of Charge (SoC):** The equivalent of a fuel gauge for the battery pack in an EV, indicating the remaining electrical capacity expressed as a percentage of its maximum capacity.
* **Power Distribution Unit (PDU):** The high-voltage junction system responsible for distributing DC electrical power from the primary battery pack to auxiliary systems and traction inverters.
* **On-Board Charger (OBC):** An electronic system that converts incoming Alternating Current (AC) from a charging station into Direct Current (DC) to replenish the high-voltage battery pack.

---

## 3. Pre-requisite Concepts

### Three-Phase EV Drivetrain Topologies
Modern high-performance EVs utilize high-voltage DC architectures tied to independent traction systems. Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFET or Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor (IGBT) inverters operate as bidirectional DC-AC converters. In traction mode, they convert the battery's DC voltage into 3-phase AC voltage to power the permanent magnet synchronous motors. During braking, they act as synchronous rectifiers to harvest kinetic energy through regenerative braking.

### The CAN Bus Vulnerability Vector
The CAN bus network uses a differential shared-wire topology prioritizing message identifiers rather than node tracking. Because it lacks native cryptographic authentication or source verification, any node connected to the network can read all traffic and broadcast spoofed frames. If an attacker gains access to a peripheral bus node, they can manipulate primary drivetrains by injecting false command inputs.

---

## 4. New Concepts Covered in the Experiment

### High-Voltage Charging Port Communication Vulnerabilities
Modern EVs utilize advanced protocols like ISO 15118 and DIN 70121 to handle parameters over Power Line Communication (PLC) lines via the Control Pilot (CP) pin. Because the Charging Port establishes a direct data connection to internal networks to coordinate charging constraints with the Battery Management System (BMS), a rogue charging station can bypass physical network barriers to inject malicious CAN packets directly onto internal vehicle lines.

### Automotive Network Cryptographic Defenses
To isolate and neutralize rogue frame injections, engineers implement multi-layered vehicle network architectures:
* **Basic Payload Encryption:** Encrypts raw CAN data fields to obscure node identifiers from unauthorized eavesdropping.
* **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS):** Network monitors that log frame patterns, flagging frequency anomalies or protocol violations, such as high packet-per-second DoS surges.
* **Secure Onboard Communication (SecOC):** An AUTOSAR framework standard that appends a Message Authentication Code (MAC) and a fresh freshness counter to each frame header, ensuring cryptographic integrity and protection against replay attacks.

## 5. Governed Formulas and Mathematical Logic

### 1. Total Electrical Power Draw (DC Bus)
The net electrical power ($P_{\text{elec}}$) drawn from or returned to the high-voltage battery pack is calculated from measured bus voltage ($V_{\text{bat}}$) and current loop measurements ($I_{\text{bat}}$):

$$P_{\text{elec}} = \frac{V_{\text{bat}} \times I_{\text{bat}}}{1000} \text{ kW}$$

* **Application:** This formula gauges the total power draw of the high-voltage system. Under a Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) auxiliary overload attack, an unauthorized increase in $I_{\text{bat}}$ highlights power draining maliciously into auxiliary circuits.

### 2. Mechanical Power Output (Motors)
The combined mechanical output power ($P_{\text{mech}}$) generated by the electric traction motors is a function of the operational shaft torque ($T_{\text{motor}}$) and angular shaft speed ($N_{\text{motor}}$):

$$P_{\text{mech}} = \frac{T_{\text{motor}} \times N_{\text{motor}}}{9549} \text{ kW}$$

* **Application:** Evaluated for both front and rear axles to determine combined mechanical output. During a replay injection attack, malicious command acceleration overrides create an uncontrolled spike in $T_{\text{motor}}$ and $N_{\text{motor}}$, causing runaway vehicle acceleration.

### 3. Overall Powertrain Conversion Efficiency
The system efficiency ($\eta$) tracks electrical and mechanical power variations dependent on the vehicle's specific operational mode:

$$\eta_{\text{Traction}} = \frac{P_{\text{mech}}}{P_{\text{elec}}} \times 100\% \quad \mid \quad \eta_{\text{Regen}} = \frac{P_{\text{elec}}}{P_{\text{mech}}} \times 100\% \quad \mid \quad \eta_{\text{Charging}} = \frac{P_{\text{bat\_input}}}{P_{\text{grid\_input}}} \times 100\%$$

* **Application:** Quantifies internal copper, iron, and switching losses. Cyber attacks that flood the CAN bus distort this value due to packet dropouts and corrupted telemetry readings.

### 4. Battery State of Charge (SoC) Depletion Rate
The battery pack depletion rate over time is calculated relative to total nominal cell capacity ($Q_{\text{cap}} = 100 \text{ Ah}$):

$$\frac{d(SoC)}{dt} = -\frac{I_{\text{bat}}}{3600 \times Q_{\text{cap}}} \times 100\% \text{ per second}$$

* **Application:** Quantifies real-time energy drainage. Attack scripts can inject deceptive values to spoof the dashboard UI, displaying a false low state of charge to force unexpected vehicle reactions.
