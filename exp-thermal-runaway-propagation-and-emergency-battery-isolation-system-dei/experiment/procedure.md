### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Set Initial Battery Temperature**
   - Adjust baseline pack temperature slider (25°C to 60°C).

2. **Step 2: Set Cooling System Efficiency**
   - Set liquid cooling efficiency slider (0% to 100%).

3. **Step 3: Trigger Thermal Fault**
   - Click 'Trigger Thermal Fault' to initiate runaway in Cell 1.

4. **Step 4: Monitor Isolation Response & Propagation**
   - Observe EBIS response time (ms) and number of affected neighboring cells.
