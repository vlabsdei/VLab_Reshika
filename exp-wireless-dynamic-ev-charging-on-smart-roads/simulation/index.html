How the Math Works

Check out these formulas! They show how our simulation calculates efficiency, power, and total energy in real-time as you adjust settings:

1. Efficiency Formula (\(\eta\))
% Rendered LaTeX

Calculates the percentage of power transferred based on how far off-center (x) the car is and how high (z) the car sits.

2. Received Power Formula (\(P_{\text{received}}\))
% Rendered LaTeX

Shows how much of the original 120 kW transmitted power actually reaches the car's battery.

3. Total Energy Captured Formula (\(\Delta E\))
% Rendered LaTeX

Integrates the power received over the time the car spends directly over the road coils to find total energy captured: \(\Delta E = \int P_{\text{received\_inst}} \cdot dt\)

How Wireless Charging on Smart Roads Works

Quick Intro Guide

1. Magnetic Induction & Asphalt

Power coils are buried right under the road concrete. Hence they are topped with a layer of asphalt. Asphalt is an insulator, which means it stops electrical current from passing through. The magnetic fields however pass straight through the asphalt to the car's pickup plate without losing power or heating up the road.

2. Smart Coils (Only ON When Needed)

The copper coils in the road are spaced 2.8 meters apart. To save electricity and avoid constant radiation, sensors in the road turn on ONLY when the car is directly over a particular coil. The coils behind and ahead stay completely off.

3. Perfecting Your Setup

The golden plate under the car catches the magnetic fields and turns them into electricity. To get the fastest charge, keep the car centered (low offset) and as close to the road as possible (low clearance).
