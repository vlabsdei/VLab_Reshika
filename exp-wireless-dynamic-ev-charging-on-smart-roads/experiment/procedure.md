### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Vehicle Profile Selection**
   - Select vehicle clearance profile: Sedan (5-13 cm), SUV (13-20 cm), or Delivery Van (20-30 cm).

2. **Step 2: Alignment Setup**
   - Set Lateral Alignment Offset slider ($x$ from 0 to 30 cm) and Vehicle Speed ($v$ from 10 to 100 km/h).

3. **Step 3: Run Dynamic Road Integration**
   - Monitor radar crosshair alignment and real-time coupling efficiency.

4. **Step 4: Energy Capture Validation**
   - Track accumulated battery energy gain ($\Delta E$ in kJ) up to maximum pack capacity (1000 kJ).
