### Theory

#### 1. Inductive Power Transfer (IPT)
Dynamic wireless charging transfers electromagnetic power from embedded road coils to vehicle receiver pads across an air gap ($z$):

$$\eta_{\text{coupling}} = \eta_{\text{max}} \cdot \exp(-k_x x^2) \cdot \exp(-k_z z)$$

Where $\eta_{\text{max}} = 90\%$, $k_x = 0.002$, $k_z = 0.03$.

#### 2. Power Transmission & Energy Capture
$$P_{\text{received}} = P_{\text{transmitted}} \cdot \left(\frac{\eta_{\text{coupling}}}{100}\right)$$

$$\Delta E = \int P_{\text{received}}(t) \, dt$$
