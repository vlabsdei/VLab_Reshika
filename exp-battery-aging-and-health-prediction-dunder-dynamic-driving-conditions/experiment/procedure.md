### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Set Thermal & C-Rate Parameters**
   - Adjust ambient temperature (25°C to 55°C) and C-rate (1C to 3C).

2. **Step 2: Driving Pattern Selection**
   - Select Normal Commute or Extreme Track pattern.

3. **Step 3: Run Lifetime Cycling**
   - Simulate 100 to 2000 discharge/charge cycles.

4. **Step 4: Analyze SOH Degradation Curve**
   - Observe End-of-Life (70% SOH) threshold boundary on the analytical graph.
