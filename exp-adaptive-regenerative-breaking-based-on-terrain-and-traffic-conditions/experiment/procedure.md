### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Set Initial Parameters**
   - Select the desired baseline Vehicle Speed (20 to 120 km/h).
   - Adjust the Road Surface Condition (Dry, Wet, or Slippery Ice).
   - Set the Traffic Congestion Level (Light, Moderate, Heavy).

2. **Step 2: Execute Braking Cycle**
   - Adjust the Driver Braking Force slider (> 150 N).
   - Observe the real-time energy harvesting efficiency meter on the telemetry panel.

3. **Step 3: Analyze Stability & Traction**
   - Monitor the 2D canvas for tire slip indications (red tire contours).
   - Evaluate the State of Charge (SOC) gain percentage.

4. **Step 4: Mathematical Verification**
   - Navigate to Phase 2 to compare theoretical vs simulated energy recovery metrics.
