### Procedure

1. **Step 1: Grid Voltage and Current Setup**
   - Set Grid Voltage (180 V to 260 V) and Current limit (5 A to 60 A).

2. **Step 2: Initiate Cable Connection**
   - Click 'Position Cable to Begin' to synchronize bidirectional inverter.

3. **Step 3: Toggle Operation Mode**
   - Select V2G Discharging or G2V Charging.

4. **Step 4: Observe BMS Safety Override**
   - Monitor automatic mode flip when SOC reaches the 50% safety boundary.
