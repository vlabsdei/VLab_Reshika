### Procedure

1. **Step 1: System Power Initialization**
   - Click center console power button to start motor idle state (500 RPM).

2. **Step 2: Thermal & Drag Load Control**
   - Adjust Cooling Vent valve (`+` / `-` keys) and Brake Force (`W` / `S` keys).

3. **Step 3: Noise Shield Deployment**
   - Press Spacebar or tap floating canvas badge to engage EMI Filter Shield.

4. **Step 4: Predictive Fault Trip Evaluation**
   - Monitor temperature (>118°C) and efficiency (<80%) safety trip boundaries.
